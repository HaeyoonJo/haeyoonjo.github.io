## 6. Komparativ und Superlativ

German adjectives have three forms:

| Form | German | English |
|---|---|---|
| Positive (Positiv) | klein | small |
| Comparative (Komparativ) | kleiner | smaller |
| Superlative (Superlativ) | am kleinsten | the smallest |

### 6.1 The Two Steps: Formation + Declension

It is important to distinguish between:

- **Comparative / Superlative formation:** Changes the adjective itself.
- **Adjective declension:** Adds an ending depending on the article, case, gender, and number.

#### Step 1: Form the comparative or superlative

| Form | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | Basic adjective | klein |
| Comparative | adjective + `-er` | klein → kleiner |
| Superlative | adjective + `-(e)st` | klein → kleinst- |

#### Step 2: Add the adjective ending

When the adjective comes **before a noun**, it needs an additional declension ending.

For example:

| Form | Breakdown | Complete Phrase |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | klein + e | der kleine Hund |
| Comparative | kleiner + e | der kleinere Hund |
| Superlative | kleinst + e | der kleinste Hund |

The final `-e` is the adjective declension ending. It is not part of the comparative or superlative formation.

### 6.2 Comparative (Komparativ)

**Formation:** adjective + `-er`

Examples:

| Positive | Comparative |
|---|---|
| klein | kleiner |
| groß | größer |
| schnell | schneller |
| schön | schöner |
| alt | älter |

When used before a noun, add the adjective declension ending.

#### Examples with Different Articles and Cases

| Case | Masculine |
|---|---|
| Nominativ | der kleiner**e** Hund |
| Akkusativ | den kleiner**en** Hund |
| Dativ | dem kleiner**en** Hund |
| Genitiv | des kleiner**en** Hundes |

With an indefinite article:

| Case | Masculine |
|---|---|
| Nominativ | ein kleiner**er** Hund |
| Akkusativ | einen kleiner**en** Hund |
| Dativ | einem kleiner**en** Hund |
| Genitiv | eines kleiner**en** Hundes |

More examples:

- Der kleinere Hund ist süß.  
  (The smaller dog is cute.)

- Ich sehe einen kleineren Hund.  
  (I see a smaller dog.)

- Das ist ein größeres Auto.  
  (That is a bigger car.)

**Remember:**

- `kleiner` = comparative form
- `kleinere` = comparative + declension ending `-e`
- `kleineren` = comparative + declension ending `-en`
- `kleineres` = comparative + declension ending `-es`

### 6.3 Superlative (Superlativ)

**Formation:** adjective + `-(e)st`

Examples:

| Positive | Superlative Stem |
|---|---|
| klein | kleinst- |
| schnell | schnellst- |
| schön | schönst- |
| groß | größt- |
| heiß | heißest- |
| alt | ältest- |

When used before a noun, add the adjective declension ending.

#### Examples with Different Genders

| Gender | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Masculine | der kleinste Hund | the smallest dog |
| Feminine | die kleinste Katze | the smallest cat |
| Neuter | das kleinste Haus | the smallest house |
| Plural | die kleinsten Hunde | the smallest dogs |

#### Examples with Different Cases (Masculine)

| Case | Example |
|---|---|
| Nominativ | der kleinste Hund |
| Akkusativ | den kleinsten Hund |
| Dativ | dem kleinsten Hund |
| Genitiv | des kleinsten Hundes |

**Remember:**

- `kleinst-` = superlative stem
- `kleinste` = superlative + declension ending `-e`
- `kleinsten` = superlative + declension ending `-en`

### 6.4 Attributive vs. Predicative Superlative

There are two important ways to use the superlative.

**A. Attributive: Before a noun**

Use the superlative stem + adjective declension ending.

| German | English |
|---|---|
| der kleinste Hund | the smallest dog |
| die schönste Stadt | the most beautiful city |
| das größte Haus | the biggest house |

**B. Predicative / Adverbial: Without an adjective directly before a noun**

Use `am + adjective + -(e)sten`.

| German | English |
|---|---|
| Der Hund ist am kleinsten. | The dog is the smallest. |
| Die Stadt ist am schönsten. | The city is the most beautiful. |
| Das Haus ist am größten. | The house is the biggest. |

**Important difference:**

- `der kleinste Hund` → before a noun; adjective declension is required.
- `Der Hund ist am kleinsten.` → after `sein`; use the `am ... -sten` form, without adjective declension.

### 6.5 Irregular Comparative and Superlative Forms

Some adjectives change their vowels or have irregular forms.

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| gut | besser | am besten |
| viel | mehr | am meisten |
| gern | lieber | am liebsten |
| hoch | höher | am höchsten |
| nah | näher | am nächsten |
| groß | größer | am größten |
| alt | älter | am ältesten |

Examples:

- Das ist ein besserer Film.  
  (That is a better movie.)

- Er ist der älteste Bruder.  
  (He is the oldest brother.)

- Das ist das größte Haus.  
  (That is the biggest house.)

### 6.6 Quick Summary

| What do you want to do? | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Say "small" | Basic adjective | klein |
| Say "smaller" | Add `-er` | kleiner |
| Say "the smallest" before a noun | Superlative stem + declension ending | der kleinste Hund |
| Say "the smallest" without a noun | `am` + superlative + `-en` | am kleinsten |

**The most important rule:**

Comparative and superlative formation does not replace adjective declension.

You still need to consider the article, case, gender, and number whenever the adjective comes before a noun.
