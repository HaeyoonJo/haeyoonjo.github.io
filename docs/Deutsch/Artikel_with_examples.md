Question: Wer? / Was?

|---|---|---|

| F | Die Frau arbeitet. | The woman is working. |
| Pl | Die Kinder spielen. | The children are playing. |

### 6. Important Grammar Notes

#### 1. Masculine and Neuter Nouns in Genitiv

Masculine and neuter nouns usually receive `-s` or `-es` in Genitiv.

Examples:
- der Mann → des Mannes
- das Kind → des Kindes
- der Tisch → des Tisches

#### 2. Plural Nouns in Dativ

Plural nouns usually receive an additional `-n` in Dativ if they do not already end in `-n` or `-s`.

Examples:
- die Kinder → den Kindern
- die Frauen → den Frauen
- die Autos → den Autos

#### 3. Indefinite Articles Have No Plural

There is no plural form of `ein` or `eine`.

Instead, use words such as:
- keine Kinder (no children)
- viele Kinder (many children)
- einige Kinder (some children)

### 8. Learning Summary

| Kasus | Main Question | Typical Function |
|---|---|---|
| Nominativ | Wer? / Was? | Subject |
| Akkusativ | Wen? / Was? | Direct Object |
| Dativ | Wem? | Indirect Object / Dativ Object |
| Genitiv | Wessen? | Possession |

**Remember:**

- `der / die / das` = Definite Articles
- `ein / eine` = Indefinite Articles
- `dies-` = Demonstrative Articles
- `welch-` = Interrogative Articles

**Important:** The articles `dies-` and `welch-` have the same endings as definite articles.

Learn the definite article table first. It provides the foundation for understanding the declension of `dies-` and `welch-`.
